# Reflection: Unit 7 – Solve a Problem, Serve a Purpose

## Lesson 1 – United Nations & Global Goals
In this lesson, I learned about the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). These goals address global challenges such as poverty, education, gender equality, clean water, and climate action. I reflected on how these goals connect to my own community and how design and technology can help make a difference.

## Lesson 2 – Apply Design Thinking
We applied the Design Thinking Process to create a meaningful website. The five steps—Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test—guided my approach.

## What I Made
My web page includes:
- A section about local recycling guidelines
- A list of upcoming sustainability events
- Contact info for local environmental organizations
- A green color theme to reflect nature and care

## What I Learned
This project taught me how web design can help solve real-world problems. By listening to a community member and aligning the website with a Global Goal, I understood how to make design meaningful and purposeful.

I also practiced HTML and CSS in a real-world context, improving my skills while also thinking about impact.
