# Reflection: Unit 6 – Feedback and Iteration (Client)

## Lesson 1 – Feedback and Iteration
In this lesson, I learned how important it is to gather feedback during the design process. Feedback helps identify what works and what needs improvement. For my cat-themed client page, I asked both classmates and the "client" (simulated by ChatGPT) to review my work. Their suggestions helped me understand the user’s needs better.

## Lesson 2 – Adding and Styling Links
I added an external link to a cat-related website (`catster.com`) and styled it using CSS. I made the link bold with a hover animation to enhance interactivity and provide users with more resources. This helped make the webpage more engaging and professional.

## Lesson 3 – Polishing Your Web Page
I added polish by styling images with borders, rounded corners, and shadows. I also used consistent colors and spacing to create a clean and cozy feeling. This included a styled photo of a cat and the use of a favicon to make the tab icon more personalized.

## Lesson 4 – Gathering Feedback From a Client
I created a list of questions for the client, such as their first impression, readability, and design preferences. Based on their feedback, I:
- Increased the font size for better readability
- Added images and names for the client’s three cats: Mimi, Tiger, and Luna
- Included a personal story about how Mimi was adopted
- Added hover animations to links for better user interaction

## What I Learned
This unit taught me that design is not just about what I like—it's about solving the user's problem. Iterating based on feedback helped me create a more personalized, functional, and visually appealing website.
