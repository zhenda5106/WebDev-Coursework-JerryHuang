Mimi's story

Mimi was once a stray cat living under a car in our neighborhood. She was scared and thin. One rainy night, we brought her some food and gave her a warm blanket. Slowly, she began to trust us. After a few weeks, she walked into our house on her own, and never left. Now she is the queen of our home.
